Last weekend, I decided to spend a quiet afternoon at home. The weather was beautifull, so I opened the window and made some coffee. I wanted to finish a book I had started a few days earlier, but I kept getting distracted by my phone.

After about an hour, I finally put my phone away and went back to reading. The story was very diffrent from what I expected, and I couldn't stop reading. I was so interested that I forgot to eat lunch.

In the afternoon, my freind sent me a message asking if I wanted to go for a walk. I told her I would be ready in twenty minutes, but I had to change my clothes first. I always leave everything until the last minite.

Before leaving, I checked the weather again because I didn't want to get caught in the rain. The forecast said it would be sunny, but I didn't completly trust it. I grabbed an umbrella anyway and left the house.

On the way to the park, we stopped at a small bakery. I wanted to buy a sandwich, but they had already sold out. We decided to get something sweet instead. The chocolate cake looked delicous, so we bought a slice and shared it.

We spent the next hour talking about school, holidays, games, and everything else we could think of. It was a simple afternoon, but somehow it became one of the most memorible days of the month.

When I got home, I realized I had left my umbrella at the park. I didn't want to go back, so I decided to look for it tomorow. I wrote a note to remind myself, because I knew I would forget.

Before going to bed, I read a few more pages. The ending was surprizing, and I immediately wanted to start another book. I was exausted by then, so I turned off the light and went to sleep.
